Calculation method and limitations

Geometry and mass

V is the volume of copper only. The fill factor k converts the winding envelope to copper volume. M = ρCuV + ρcoreVcore; the second term is zero for the air coil.

All dimensions are converted to metres internally. Mass is displayed in grams. Resistivity is in Ω·m and density in kg/m³.

Electrical model

P = ρ m² ℓ² / (V S² K²)

a = ρ m ℓ / (S K U)
d = 2√(a / π)
N = V / (ℓ a)
R = ρ N ℓ / a; I = U / R

m: target dipole moment (A·m²), ℓ: mean turn length (m), S: magnetic area (m²), a: bare conductor area (m²), ρ: electrical resistivity (Ω·m), U: voltage (V), K: core gain.

Scope and references

Wire size and turn count are continuous estimates, not a winding specification. Choose an available insulated wire, an integer turn count and verify the finished coil separately. No temperature rise, driver loss, inductive transient, end-turn correction or thermal limit is calculated.
